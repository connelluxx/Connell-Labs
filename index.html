About Connell Labs

Growth is not an accident. It's a strategy.

Connell Labs is a performance marketing and customer acquisition agency for automotive and real estate businesses in Nigeria.

Our story

Built for businesses where one customer changes the month.

Most businesses selling cars or property already advertise online. Many are paying for reach and engagement that never turns into a showroom visit or a property inspection. The gap is rarely the product. It is the system between the ad and the sale.

Connell Labs was set up to close that gap. We plan who should see your ads, what they should say, where interested people should go next and how your team should receive them. Then we measure it and improve it.

We chose to focus on automotive and real estate because they share the same challenge: high-value decisions, long consideration and buyers who want to trust who they deal with.
